# Story beats — Cynthia's Crown

## Setting

Kanto, 20 years after FRLG. Familiar towns, modernized buildings, expanded Silph, redesigned Indigo Plateau. Post-game opens a similarly future-shifted Johto.

## Cast roles

- **Player**: aspiring monotype trainer; chooses primary type at the Type Shrine in Pallet.
- **Main rival**: standard FRLG-style competitive rival (Blue's successor — *name TBD*). Hostile-friendly arc, no monotype restriction.
- **Cynthia**: secondary rival — friendly, mythologically inclined, restarting her journey in Kanto. Recurring appearances:
  1. Pallet Town intro (post-type-select) — frames the journey.
  2. Mt. Moon — lore drop on type symbolism.
  3. Celadon — first formal battle vs Cynthia (scaled to player level).
  4. Saffron — assists during Silph plot beat.
  5. Victory Road — second formal battle.
  6. Post-Champion ceremony — passes the torch and unlocks Johto.
  7. Post-game Johto — multi-stage finale around the Sinjoh-style ruin update.

## Type-mastery thread

Each Gym arc earns a "Type Insight" — short cutscene with Cynthia or a regional sage commenting on the chosen type's mythology. These insights stack into a journal item.
